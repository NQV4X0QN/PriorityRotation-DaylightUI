# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

The first release: a one-button rotation for WoW: Forever, built on DaylightUI.

### Added

- Rotation — **one key steps through a list of abilities you build.** Up to 10 spells, macros and trinkets, with Frequency, After (cast right after a channelled spell) and Combat only for each, auto-target on death for each specialization, and a separate list for each character and specialization. Open it with /pr
- Rotation — **drawn in DaylightUI's look**, with its settings in the DaylightUI options window under Plugins, Rotation
- Error Messages — **keep chosen groups of red error messages off the screen**, and their voice lines quiet: combat, spells and cooldowns, items and bags, mail and vendors, looting, mounts and flight paths. Works alongside DaylightUI's own error settings
- Sounds — **silence chosen game sounds**, from Spell Fizzle and Clock Chimes to Mount Footsteps and Cat Yawns
- Gear — **Auto Equip Upgrades** puts on better gear from your bags as you find it, for the qualities you choose. /pr equipcheck shows what it would do
- Coming from VeritasUI — **your Priority Rotation spell lists are copied over** the first time, while VeritasUI's Priority Rotation is still on
